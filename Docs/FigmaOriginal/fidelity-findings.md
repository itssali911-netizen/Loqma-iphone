## Why direct image comparison needs separate scope labels

1. Native screenshots are1320×2868 iPhone16ProMax frames; their logical view is440×956 points at3×. The source board is a montage of English previews, not an exported app framebuffer from that same device. The Settings raster4096×1550 also differs from the board5899×2232.288 geometry. Crop coordinates and scale conversions must therefore be explicit.
2. Arabic Tajawal/RTL and real food names/macros do not have the same glyph bounds/content as English Work Sans and fictional fitness-feature/profile text. Same-data English comparison fixtures and Arabic behavior captures serve different purposes; neither can be silently substituted for the other.
3. Existing native captures are baseline evidence for build59. A diagnostic overlay of a cropped source phone and this baseline can reveal a gross hierarchy mismatch, but cannot prove newly implemented code or exact original fonts/constraints/variants.
4. Differences in functional domains must be called out. Date/AI filters, coaching, live chat, accounts, referral and linked-watch flows are not made functional by copying static screenshots. Existing food/backup/analysis controls must not disappear solely because the available image board has no counterpart.
5. This search/settings comparison inspected two of the three original full-board assets in the evidence package. There is no evidence in these two contexts that the separate original icon, magnifier illustration, avatar, watch image or child phone frame can be exported individually with original vector/constraint properties. The visible content exists; the missing part is addressable engineering detail and independent source assets, not the visual presence of these screens.

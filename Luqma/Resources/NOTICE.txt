This cache is not the full Open Food Facts database. Live search and barcode lookups use Open Food Facts; previously saved OFF products work offline. Only complete as-sold packaging/manufacturer nutrition is accepted. Missing nutrients are not zero-filled, and no density or recipe nutrition is inferred. Source content can contain mistakes; review the package label.
Photo recognition categories retain their stable category IDs, names and illustrative gram references for identifying components; their old nutritional values have been removed from the application. A detected component requires an OFF product or explicitly entered personal food before logging. Legacy nutritional fixtures remain in the test source only and are not bundled with the iPhone app.
Private manual foods and past diary entries are user data, preserved separately from the external food database.
Similarity scores and calorie ranges are not calibrated confidence intervals.
